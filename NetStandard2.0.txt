LIST Of Types that will not compile in a netstandard2.0/ne48 build:

Entire XQuinn.IO.Finders namespace (uses EnumerationOptions)
Entire XQuinn.Reflection.IL namespace (relies heavily on BytesLittleEndian)
Entire XQuinn.NetConsole.Apps namespace (relies heavily on Finders, Super private anyways, not for outsiders to use ever.)
BytesLittleEndian (relies on binaryprimitives, i dont feel like making a byeteconverter edition)
DynamicInvoker (relies on assemblyloadcontext from net6)
InvokerInterface(relies on dynamic invoker)
